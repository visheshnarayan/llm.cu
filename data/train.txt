The capital of France is Paris. Paris is located in northern France along the Seine River. It is the most populous city in France and serves as the country's political, economic, and cultural center. The Eiffel Tower, built in 1889, stands 330 meters tall and is one of the most recognizable structures in the world. The Louvre Museum in Paris houses over 35,000 works of art including the Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci. Notre-Dame Cathedral, located on the Île de la Cité, is a masterpiece of Gothic architecture. The Palace of Versailles, built for King Louis XIV, is located just outside Paris and features the famous Hall of Mirrors. Paris hosted the Olympic Games in 1900, 1924, and 2024. The city is known for its cuisine, fashion, and art. French cuisine includes dishes like croissants, baguettes, coq au vin, and crème brûlée.

The capital of Germany is Berlin. Berlin is located in northeastern Germany along the Spree River. It is the largest city in Germany by both area and population. The Brandenburg Gate, built in 1791, is a neoclassical monument and one of the most well-known landmarks of Berlin. The Berlin Wall, which divided the city from 1961 to 1989, was a symbol of the Cold War. The Reichstag building houses the German parliament and features a famous glass dome designed by architect Norman Foster. Berlin is known for its vibrant arts scene, museums, and nightlife. The Museum Island in Berlin contains five world-renowned museums. German cuisine includes dishes like bratwurst, sauerkraut, pretzels, and Black Forest cake.

The capital of Japan is Tokyo. Tokyo is located on the eastern coast of Honshu island along Tokyo Bay. It is the most populous metropolitan area in the world with over 37 million people. The Tokyo Tower, built in 1958 and inspired by the Eiffel Tower, stands 332 meters tall. Mount Fuji, the highest peak in Japan at 3,776 meters, is visible from Tokyo on clear days. The Imperial Palace in Tokyo is the primary residence of the Emperor of Japan. Tokyo hosted the Summer Olympic Games in 1964 and 2021. The city is known for its advanced technology, manga and anime culture, and traditional temples. Japanese cuisine includes sushi, ramen, tempura, and wagyu beef. The Shibuya Crossing in Tokyo is one of the busiest pedestrian intersections in the world.

The capital of the United Kingdom is London. London is located in southeastern England along the Thames River. It is one of the world's leading financial centers and a major global city. The Tower of London, built in 1078 by William the Conqueror, served as a royal palace and prison. Big Ben, the iconic clock tower at the Palace of Westminster, is a symbol of London. Buckingham Palace serves as the official residence of the British monarch. The British Museum in London holds a collection of over eight million works. London hosted the Summer Olympic Games in 1908, 1948, and 2012. The city is known for its theater district in the West End, its diverse cuisine, and its historic pubs. The London Underground, opened in 1863, is the oldest underground railway in the world.

The capital of Italy is Rome. Rome is located in central Italy along the Tiber River. It is known as the Eternal City due to its long history spanning over two and a half millennia. The Colosseum in Rome, completed in 80 AD, could hold between 50,000 and 80,000 spectators and was used for gladiatorial contests. The Vatican City, an independent city-state within Rome, is the headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church and home to Saint Peter's Basilica. The Trevi Fountain, completed in 1762, is the largest Baroque fountain in Rome. The Pantheon, built around 125 AD, is one of the best-preserved ancient Roman buildings. Rome hosted the Summer Olympic Games in 1960. Italian cuisine includes pasta, pizza, gelato, and espresso coffee.

The capital of France is Paris. Paris is the city of light and the city of love. The Seine River flows through the heart of Paris past iconic landmarks. Montmartre hill in northern Paris is home to the Sacré-Cœur Basilica. The Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous avenues in the world, stretching from the Arc de Triomphe to the Place de la Concorde. The Musée d'Orsay houses an impressive collection of Impressionist art including works by Monet, Renoir, and Degas. The Paris Catacombs contain the remains of over six million people in underground tunnels. The Centre Pompidou is a modern art museum with an unconventional inside-out architectural design. French fashion houses like Chanel, Louis Vuitton, and Dior are based in Paris.

The capital of Spain is Madrid. Madrid is located in the center of the Iberian Peninsula. It is the highest capital city in the European Union at an altitude of 667 meters. The Prado Museum in Madrid is one of the finest art museums in the world, housing works by Velázquez, Goya, and El Greco. The Royal Palace of Madrid is the official residence of the Spanish Royal Family and one of the largest palaces in Europe. The Puerta del Sol square in Madrid is the symbolic center of Spain where distances are measured from. The Santiago Bernabéu Stadium is the home of Real Madrid, one of the most successful football clubs in the world. Spanish cuisine includes paella, tapas, tortilla española, and churros with chocolate.

The capital of China is Beijing. Beijing is located in northern China and has served as the capital of China for much of the last eight centuries. The Forbidden City in Beijing was the Chinese imperial palace from the Ming dynasty to the end of the Qing dynasty and is the world's largest palace complex. The Great Wall of China, one of the greatest architectural feats in human history, passes through the mountains north of Beijing. Tiananmen Square in Beijing is one of the largest city squares in the world. The Temple of Heaven is a complex of religious buildings where emperors of the Ming and Qing dynasties would pray for good harvests. Beijing hosted the Summer Olympic Games in 2008 and the Winter Olympic Games in 2022. Chinese cuisine includes Peking duck, dim sum, hot pot, and dumplings.
